# PathSense 房屋版海报

横版使用左对齐文字与靠右的图标，画布为 3840 × 2160。竖版使用居中文字与图标，画布为 2160 × 3840。两张都使用纯白背景、macOS 系统字体及同一枚房屋图标。图标的墨色在横版以 10.4% 不透明度叠印，在竖版以 9.2% 不透明度叠印。

## 海报文案

```text
PathSense
The signs are there.
Now hear them.
Find your way inside unfamiliar buildings.
```

## 图标绘制

图标由内置 ImageGen 参照 Apple「旁白」图形迭代生成，海报用 Core Text 独立排字。源图形见 [Apple 辅助功能页面的 VoiceOver 图标](https://www.apple.com/v/accessibility/ae/images/shared/icon_voiceover__fd6trvpl5nmi_large_2x.png)。

最终图标采用以下提示词。

> Edit the input PathSense pictogram, which is the current master. The user needs to immediately read the outdoor silhouette as a HOUSE, even at 24 px. Keep the existing reassuring semibold stroke weight, the internal standing VoiceOver person with outstretched arms, and the small lower-left speaker with two sound waves. Make the outer silhouette midway between angular and rounded, strongly akin to the crisp 'house' glyph in the Apple SF Symbols system. ONLY ONE front-facing flat monochrome graphic on true transparency, centered with a little breathing room. Key geometric edit: a CLASSIC GABLED ROOF, two substantial perfectly STRAIGHT diagonal roof slopes meeting at a well-defined apex at 40 degrees, with just a small smooth fillet radius at the peak. Each roof slope extends visibly past the vertical wall at both eaves (overhang approx one stroke width). Both walls are straight and vertical. The floor is flat. Small clean radii at the wall-floor corner and rounded stroke caps, about 3–4% of total icon width; this must read like a gable, not a dome, arch, capsule, bubble, or shield. Let the long clean diagonals do the work. The stroke is sturdy medium-bold as in the reference, approx 7% of mark width, only marginally lighter than reference if necessary. The two short symmetrical projecting eaves are strong, simple cues. Do not add any chimney, roof texture, windows or literal door. The person stays comfortably centered in the house, the speaker floats in the deliberate break in the lower-left wall/floor, with negative space surrounding it. Keep the original gesture and only adjust spacing optically as needed. Black ink and genuine transparent negative space. No outside rounded-square tile, no circle, no background, no gradients, highlights, drop shadows, lettering or wordmark.
